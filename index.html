01 — The name

One name. Three readings. The first is a path.

i

Intelligence at rest

A model that answers, predicts, completes — powerful, but waiting to be asked. The lowercase i is also the individual: the I on whose behalf everything that follows will act.

Agent

The hinge

An agent perceives, decides and acts toward an objective. Agency is the moment intelligence stops being a reply and starts being a consequence.

Si

Superintelligence

Capability beyond the human range. One possible route is the accumulation of capabilities through systems that act, learn and coordinate; this is not proof that superintelligence will emerge.

Second reading — the plural

iAgentSi

Look again. Inside iAgentSi stands the plural — Agents — framed by an i on either side. One possible future is a society of agents, each standing between an intelligence that delegates and an intelligence that acts.

Third reading — the condition

si = if si = yes

In French, si introduces a condition and can answer a negative question affirmatively; in Spanish, si means if and sí means yes. Condition and consent — the two questions every autonomous agent must settle before it acts on someone's behalf.

02 — The agentic thesis

Six propositions.

  1. IIntelligence that only answers has potential; agency gives it an additional route to consequence.
  2. IIAgency is the conversion of intelligence into consequence.
  3. IIIAgents that act, observe and improve may offer a path toward superintelligence, but coordination alone does not establish it.
  4. IVIf superintelligence emerges, people and institutions may interact with it through agents of their own.
  5. VThe agent therefore becomes the unit that matters: of capability, of delegation, of accountability.
  6. VIThe design of the agent layer may shape how humanity accesses advanced intelligence — and how advanced intelligence affects humanity.

A conceptual thesis, not a forecast, validated scientific theory or operating product.

The central question

If superintelligence emerges, how will other intelligences interact with it?

Agency is one possible mediation layer: intentions and delegated tasks flow outward; results, evidence and accountability flow back. This exchange is the meaning of the motion identity.

03 — The path

From i to SI: four stages of agency.

Stage 01 · i

Respond

Intelligence answers. A prompt in, an output out. Its reach ends where the person asking stops.

Stage 02 · Agent

Act

Intelligence receives tools, memory and an objective, and executes on someone's behalf.

Stage 03 · Agents

Coordinate

Agents delegate to agents. Work is decomposed, distributed and recombined beyond any single mind's span.

Stage 04 · SI

Compound

Every cycle of action informs the next. Capability may compound; whether it exceeds human intelligence is an open empirical question.

04 — The category

Agentic Super­intelligence.

A·gentic · S·uper · I·ntelligence → ASI

Names in this field usually pick one side — the agent or the superintelligence. iAgentSi holds both ends of the arc, and the hinge between them.

That positions it as a potential category identity rather than a feature name. It can carry a product, a platform, a research program or a standard, and it can retain its meaning as the field evolves. The hypothesized transition is already written into it.

Here, Agentic Superintelligence names a proposed conceptual positioning, not an established category or a replacement for the conventional meaning of ASI (Artificial Superintelligence).
